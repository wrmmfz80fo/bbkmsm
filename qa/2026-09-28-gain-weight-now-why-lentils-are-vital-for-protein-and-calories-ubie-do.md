Why Lentils Are Important for Vegan Bulking

Adopting a vegan bulking plan means maximizing plant-based proteins and calories to support muscle growth. Lentils stand out as one of the most efficient, cost‐effective, and nutrient‐dense foods you can include. Below, we'll explore why lentils are important for vegan bulking, how to use them strategically, and practical tips for getting the most out of every serving.

Nutritional Powerhouse: What's in a Cup of Lentils?

Lentils deliver a robust mix of macronutrients and micronutrients critical for anyone looking to bulk on a vegan diet. Here's a snapshot of what one cooked cup (about 198 g) provides:

Calories: 230 kcal

Protein: 18 g

Carbohydrates: 40 g

Dietary Fiber: 16 g

Fat: 0.8 g

Iron: 6.6 mg (35% DV)

Folate (B9): 358 µg (90% DV)

Magnesium: 71.3 mg (18% DV)

Potassium: 731 mg (15% DV)

These values make lentils an ideal staple for vegan bulking, ensuring you hit both calorie and protein targets without relying on processed foods.
