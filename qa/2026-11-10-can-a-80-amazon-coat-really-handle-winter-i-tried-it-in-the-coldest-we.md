THIS WINTER HAS been one for the books. I can't personally remember sub-zero temperatures of this magnitude in my neck of the woods. I needed a full arsenal of winter jackets to get me through it. On the bright side, it was the perfect environment to test out a few different winter jackets so that you fine folks didn't have to.

At the peak of the cold spell, my Expedition Parka came in handy—it's by far the warmest jacket I've ever owned. But a $1,600 parka isn't always the most practical, especially if it's a quick trip to the supermarket or when Mother Nature decides to spare us a little break.

Considering I already own a pricey winter jacket, I wanted to get something a little more cost-effective and suitable for the warmer(ish) weather. Amazon Essentials has never done me wrong, and their down winter jacket just happened to be on sale for less than $40—perhaps a sign.

But could a $80 jacket from Amazon really do the trick? I tested it out over a few particularly cold weeks; here are some of my findings.

Final Thoughts on the Amazon Essentials Puffer Coat

All things considered, the Amazon Essentials puffer exceeded my expectations. It's certainly not the most stylish jacket I own, but it's great for what it is, and it's the perfect mid-sized winter jacket that gets the job done without a ton of bulk. The insulation stacks up well with other much higher-priced jackets I own, and I never felt like I needed to add another layer in sub-freezing temperatures.

I'm typically wary of sub-$100 winter jackets, but I was pleasantly surprised by this one's insulation and heat-retention capabilities. While I wouldn't necessarily recommend it as a primary winter coat for harsher climates, it's a great supplementary piece, and it's certainly durable enough for everyday use in warmer winter temperatures without a ton of precipitation. And at this price point, you'd be hard-pressed to find an option that gets the job done as effectively. Amazon for the win once again.
