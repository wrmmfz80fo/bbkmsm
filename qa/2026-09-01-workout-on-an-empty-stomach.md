# Workout on an Empty Stomach?

[on an Empty Stomach](https://therapy.bairibu.com/blog/7164506508.html)

Q: What's your opinion of working out on an empty stomach first thing in the morning?

Answer:That's a great question.

It's no big deal to workout on an empty stomach. It won't get you extra fat burning results, but it won't have any negative consequences.

[on an Empty](https://therapy.ketocarbcheck.com/blog/3588509107.html)

[an Empty](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/blog/9042536223.html)

I used to recommend a small protein-carbohydrate snack before training, but Brad Pilon's ESE program has changed my opinion on that.

I originally made the suggestion to eat a small amount of protein and carbohydrates because back in University, it wasn't uncommon to see girls come in without eating and literally faint in the gym.

But I think those instances were extreme cases.

I personally now do mini-workouts (off day workouts) 2-3 times per week before eating but after a 90minute dog walk - first thing in the morning. And there's no problem.

And like dozens of other TT members, I've also done a workout while fasting for over 18 hours (on the day I used ESE). Again, no harm.

[350 Calories Burned Fast](https://github.com/wrmmfz80fo/kvopwx/blob/main/misc/2026-08-29-350-calories-burned-fast.md)

So if you want to get up, not eat, and workout, it's fine.
