# Why Tommy Hilfiger's New Sailing Collection Is Your Summer Weekend Uniform

TOMMY HILFIGER IS to American prep what pushups are to chest day. For decades, the New York City clothing brand has turned flag sweaters, polos, chinos, and sailing jackets into menswear staples. Now, it's returning to its nautical roots with a new collection alongside the U.S. SailGP Team, serving up color-blocked rugbies, striped knits, technical polos, and performance outerwear that look just as good dockside as they do downtown.

This marks Tommy's second year outfitting the U.S. SailGP Team. If you're unfamiliar with the sport, think Formula 1 meets the America's Cup: national teams piloting hydrofoiling catamarans that can hit highway speeds while battling for points on a global circuit.

"What I love about this collection is that it merges the world of the U.S. SailGP Team on the water with everyday life in a way that feels modern while still honoring the heritage of sailing,” Mike Buckley, U.S. SailGP’s co-owner and team principal, tells Men’s Health. “It creates another lens and entry point into the sport through fashion, culture and personal style."

After testing the collection ourselves, it goes well beyond race day. The striped sweater is soft, luxe, and the perfect layer to throw over your shoulders once the sun starts to set. The cargo tech pants impressed us even more. Lightweight, water-resistant, and cut with a beautiful drape, they kept us cool and comfortable during long days in the heat, while their suite of pockets eliminated a need for a sling bag altogether. And when the wind picked up during a boat ride, the convertible jacket earned its keep.
