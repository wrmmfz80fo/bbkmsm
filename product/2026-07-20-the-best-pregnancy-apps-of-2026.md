# The Best Pregnancy Apps of 2026

These Are the 6 Pregnancy Apps We Recommend

Pregnancy comes with so many emotions, thoughts, worries, and new experiences. When I was pregnant with my first, I’d have daily questions and worries swirling in my head: Why is my body doing this new thing? Is this a normal symptom? What do I need to do to prepare? What’s that baby doing inside?

Pregnancy Baby Tracker - What to Expect

A decade or two ago, as soon as you announced your pregnancy, you’d be handed a copy of What to Expect When You’re Expecting. The trusted pregnancy brand now offers a pregnancy tracker app, which keeps you informed about your baby’s week-by-week growth and development, connects you to virtual community groups based on your due date as well as community groups based on your location, interests, medical conditions, and more, provides search tools to guide you to answers about pregnancy, parenting, and fertility; and offers access to thousands of medically accurate articles and personal stories written by parents and parents-to-be.

Once baby is here, you’ll be able to track your child’s sleep schedule and growth, get advice on feedings, and get information on development and parenting tips up through toddlerhood.

BabyCenter Pregnancy Tracker

The BabyCenter app can help you keep track of your pregnancy with 3D fetal development videos and a pregnancy calendar to remind you of your prenatal appointments and to jot down your symptoms. You’ll get access to pregnancy and health advice, become part of an online community where you can talk to other expecting parents and meet people with due dates close to yours, and get help with baby names at the Baby Names Shuffle feature.

And the app doesn’t leave you behind once baby is born. You’ll be able to log feedings even down to the detail of which breast you last fed on, take note of baby’s naps, and track your little one’s weight and growth.

The Bump App

The Bump’s Pregnancy and Baby Tracker App tracks your pregnancy and baby growth (via 3D growth simulations and the ever-popular weekly fruit comparisons), but there’s so much more available to you on this app. Weekly videos from experts show you what to expect each week of your pregnancy, and The Bump’s award-winning editorial staff provides daily content specific to your phase of pregnancy, chock-full of need-to-know info about what to expect, such as how to address morning sickness, must-haves for your hospital bag, and much more.

The baby name feature will provide you with ideas for your little one’s name, as well as the meaning and background of each name. You’ll get access to a kick counter, information about your prenatal appointments, such as important questions you may want to remember to ask your doctor or midwife, and seamless integration with your phone calendar, so you’ll never forget an appointment. You can upload pregnancy photos and baby photos to document your bump growth as well as baby’s first year. But what we like most is that you can create your baby shower registry right in the app. Add products from Amazon, Target, Walmart, and more, and integrate registries from other retailers to manage what has been purchased.

Sprout Pregnancy Tracker
