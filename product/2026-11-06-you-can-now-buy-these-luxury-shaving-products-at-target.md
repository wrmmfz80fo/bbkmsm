# You Can Now Buy These Luxury Shaving Products At Target

Luxury shave company Harry’s is teaming up with Target to make its trendy, high-quality shave products more accessible. Starting today, Harry’s products will be sold on Target.com, and from August 21 they’ll be available in Target stores nationwide.
