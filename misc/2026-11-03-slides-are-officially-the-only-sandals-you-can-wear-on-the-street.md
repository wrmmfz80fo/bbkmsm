Summer might be officially winding down, but we’re not putting away our shorts and sandals yet. Actually, we’re excited about sandals more now than ever. No, these aren’t the shower shoes you had in college. This new generation of slides is bold and elevated, featuring bright colors and big-as-life logos in keeping with the ’90s style resurgence you’d have to be blind not to notice. They’ve even made appearances on runways from designer brands like Gucci, Fendi, and Prada.

But slides aren’t just for runway models and streetwear hypebeasts. In a recent trend-finding trip to menswear trade shows PROJECT, Liberty and FN Platform, we found familiar brands like Lacoste and Puma embracing the look for next spring, too.

The best part: You can still rock this look in the city. That’s right. Slides are your ticket to freeing your dogs on the street. Why, you ask? Covering the tops of your feet instead of splaying your toes apart for the world to see is simply less jarring too look at. Add in a bold color and a logo that pops, and you have a looks that’s far less “basic bro” than a plain old pair of flops.

Most of the runway looks we’ve seen actually pair socks with slides (see above). It’s the kind of look that embraces the inherent ugliness of socks with sandals, but styles it in a way that still manages to make it look cool. But how do you make the look more style risk-taker and less what-the-hell-is-that-dude-wearing? Be sure to pick solid, crew-length socks in dark or medium neutral colors like gray, navy, or olive (not white). Picking a pair with texture or heathering (flecks of other colors woven in), the cooler it will look. It’s a style risk to be sure, but when done right your look will stand out and show off your cajones.
