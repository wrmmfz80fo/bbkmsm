There’s Game of Thrones beer from the Ommegang brewery in New York, where every brew resembles a character or theme—like Sour Blonde for Cersei. Here's another treat to crank your viewing parties up a notch: GOT-themed wine. Check out the new vintages of Game of Thrones wine, created by award-winning winemaker Bob Cabral.
