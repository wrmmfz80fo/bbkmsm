# 13 Quotes From the Most Motivational Man Alive: Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson

[Finish Your Next Workout With This Challenging Three-Minute Circuit​](https://github.com/qyc7mgni0y/iacmnbi/blob/main/misc/2026-08-29-finish-your-next-workout-with-this-challenging-three-minute-circuit.md)

Making it through your workout (or let’s be honest, even making it to the gym to do your workout) can be tough, especially at the end of the year. When you’re juggling travel and family time, and those delicious holiday treats just keep calling your name, it can seem near impossible to stay committed to your fitness regimen.

Never fear, because The Rock has got your back. Next time you feel like you can’t get through that last set of burpees, imagine Dwayne Johnson's voice, loud and proud and firing you up to finish through — and even to pump out a few extra reps for good measure, too.

These are the best motivational quotes from The Rock you can come back to, again and again, to push you through those moments of weakness during any tough workout session.
