How Should a Crewneck Sweatshirt Fit?

A crewneck sweatshirt should fit close enough to your neck and body, but not completely skin tight. You want to have a bit of movement and room to layer underneath—not to mention, breathe. But at the end of the day, how you want your crewneck sweatshirt to fit is at your discretion. Crewneck sweaters come in a few styles: classic, slim-fit, oversized.

How To Style a Crewneck Sweatshirt

Whether you're aiming for a street-ready look or taking the formal route, the best crewneck sweatshirts should add comfort and versatility to your wardrobe. Check out some simple outfit ideas you can tie in with your new crewneck.

[to Add](https://pepvora.com/t7m8fku9fs/)

Dress It Up: Layer your crewneck over a button-down shirt and pair with some pants or chinos. The collar of shirt should hang outside the sweatshirt—folded downward. This particular fit could work for the office or date night.

Keep It Casual: Chill is always good, so dark jeans and sneakers would make a laid-back look come together. This style can take you from day to night.

Make It Business Casual: Show you mean business by putting a blazer on top of your crewneck sweatshirt—as long as it's not a rugged style. Choose a dark blazer if your crewneck is light and vice versa.
