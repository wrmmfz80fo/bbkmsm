# Hotel Gym, Bodyweight, and Fat Burning Workouts

Okay, so what workout do you do when you are on the road?

Well, my advice is pretty simple. Use the hotel gym, or suck it up and go to a local gym and pay the outrageous "cover charge" to do your workout. Hey, put it on your expense account if you can.

Way back in March when I was in LA, I paid 20 bucks to train at Gold's Gym. It was worth it. Plain and simple. Heck, going to a Gold's Gym is far more entertaining to me than going to a movie. So I feel I get a bargain everytime. That's just my mindset. No cost is too high for my fitness.

You either pay a little now, or pay a lot later. Like getting your foot amputated when you're a diabetic. No thanks. I'll pay the 20 bucks now.

Later in April I stayed at the Opryland hotel in Nashville that had a better than average hotel gym. So my workout in the hotel gym was different than my normally scheduled workout due to lack of certain pieces of equipment, but here's what I did...
