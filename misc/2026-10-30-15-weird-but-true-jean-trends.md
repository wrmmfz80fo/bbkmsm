# ​15 Weird But True Jean Trends

If you didn’t fork over the money to go listen to celebrities in the desert, let us fill you in: People are wearing some pretty weird jeans to Coachella. Jeans with only one leg. Jeans made into wide-leg jorts. Jeans that look more deceased than distressed.

Denim disasters aren’t contained to festival fashion. We’ve lived through some pretty weird jean trends over the years, some of which are back in style (and others that are better left for dead). Scroll through to see what we mean with these past and modern-day horrors.

Actress Ryan Destiny stepped out at Coachella in the perfect jeans for when you really want to show off your leg day gains. Also, we get it: Denim is restrictive. Sometimes you just have to lose an entire leg of fabric to open yourself up for a full range of motion.

We have actual nightmares of what were to happen if you zipped up these new Vetements x Levis jeans too fast.

Jorts have been a stain on America’s mainstream fashion narrative since the ‘60s. Baggy black jorts with a wide brown belt though? We need to lie down. That’s next level bad. We hope Cody Simpson made these during Coachella, and didn’t actually pay someone money for them.

What did we do to deserve this denim trend? The brand, JNCO, stands for “Judge None, Choose One.” We’d like to flip the word order in those directives.

Serious question: Have you ever met a millionaire who chains their wallet to their pants? What’s the real purpose here? What’s so metal about excessively guarding your symbol of capitalism?

If we can direct your attention away from Joe Jonas and the crazy boxing outfit in the middle, you'll see that acid wash jeans have re-entered the building.

Another color of jorts, another paint trend, an earlier year of Coachella—and it’s all still bad.

These patchwork jeans are for when you have to be a scarecrow at six and then perform as the Backstreet Boys at seven.

The next generation of patchwork jeans materialized at Coachella via another old pop band connection, Victoria Beckham’s son.

This extreme fade look popped up in a music video in the early aughts, and hasn’t stopped haunting suburban malls since.

ASOS boggled the internet’s mind this year when it came out with these cropped, clear knee jeans, but we are sure they’ll be all over festivals this summer. This style is only available for women currently, but for some reason, we don’t think any of you are too bummed about that.
