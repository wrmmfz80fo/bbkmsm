# Michael Bolton Cancels Tour Dates Following Brain Cancer Diagnosis

The Grammy-winning artist Michael Bolton has announced that he will be taking some time off from performing this year in order to focus on his health, after undergoing an urgent operation in December to remove a brain tumor.

The 70-year-old singer illuminated the situation in a Facebook post on Friday that the previous year had included "some very unexpected challenges" for him.

"Just before the holidays, it was discovered that I had a brain tumor, which required immediate surgery," he wrote. "Thanks to my incredible medical team, the surgery was a success. I am now recuperating at home and surrounded by the tremendous love and support of my family."
