Evolve your style

Like a superhero catch phrase (“Hulk Smash!”), a fitted moto style leather jacket was Reynolds’ calling card. It looked cool and gave off a slightly bad-ass vibe. At the September cover shoot, he decided they had become a little predictable for him. “I feel like every 40-year-old dad is wearing that style now,” he told our fashion director, Brian Boye, as he thumbed through a rack of clothes. Instead, he opted for a textured brown varsity style jacket from Perry Ellis. The shape is on trend, but not too flashy.

Know your brands

Given a choice of about seven great denim brands to choose from, Reynolds immediately gravitated to a slim pair of Michael Kors jeans. He’s worn the brand before and he knows their fit works with his narrow waist and muscular legs. “For me, it starts from the pants,” Reynolds says. “They need to fit right. When you find a pair you like that fit you well and don’t bunch at the bottom or too baggy in the front, you can do anything. Add a t-shirt and a jacket. It’s easy from there.” Figure out your body type and the brand that works for you. Then buy them in every color.

Streamline your kicks

There’s a definite line that should not be crossed between shoes you’d wear to the gym and ones that go well with slim-cut jeans. Hint: They’re a solid color, sleek, and preferably made from leather. You should not be able to work out in them, but they should always be comfortable. “If it ain’t functional, I don’t want it,” Reynolds says. “But it helps if it’s fashionable.”

Stay scruffy, but clean

A recent Women’s Health survey found that more than half of women surveyed prefer scruff to smooth skin. That doesn’t give you license to go all Duck Dynasty and call it a day. Scruff needs to be manicured regularly, like Reynolds’ 10 o’clock shadow that naturally drifts into the fade of his sideburns and fade of his hair. It looks consistent, and effortless. Keep your beard at about a half an inch, and you’ll look rugged and refined.
