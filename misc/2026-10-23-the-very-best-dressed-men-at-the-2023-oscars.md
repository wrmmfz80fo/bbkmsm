# The Very Best-Dressed Men at the 2023 Oscars

SO THAT concludes the 95th Academy Awards, AKA the biggest night of Hollywood. Historic moments in motion pictures were made, from the major sweep of Everything Everywhere All At Once to notable acting award subs. Another groundbreaking moment: the return of tailored, grown-up menswear.Whether it's our cover stars Jonathan Majors and The Rock, or classic Hollywood dudes Hugh Grant and Samuel L. Jackson, or this year’s winners Ke Huy Quan and Brendan Fraser, these guys have come to the Oscars red carpet to say one thing: classic formalwear is back. They’ve ditched anything that is funky or slouchy or "trendy" and instead embraced elegant formal fits that they can look back on in a few decades without being embarrassed. Plus, their timeless tried-and-true fits are easy to learn and emulate for us average Joes as well.
