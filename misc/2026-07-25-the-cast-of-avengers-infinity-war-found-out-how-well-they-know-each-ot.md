# The Cast of 'Avengers: Infinity War' Found Out How Well They Know Each Other

The stars of Avengers: Infinity War had their trivia skills put to the test when they stopped by Jimmy Kimmel Live! as part of Avengers week. The questions focused on how well they knew each other — plus a little bit of Marvel Comics knowledge, of course.

Scarlett Johansson, Dave Bautista, Mark Ruffalo, Danai Gurira, and Tom Hiddleston got to play a new game called "Guess the Avenger," where they had to match one of their cast members with a story that Kimmel shared about one of them.

[The Cast of Avengers](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/)

While Kimmel pointed out that the Marvel universe now has 19 movies with Infinity War, this was the first time some of the actors had the chance to work with each other. Still, it seems like the cast had bonded together quickly in a short amount of time and could guess some of these questions correctly.

The first question ended up being about Johansson, who revealed that she accidentally flashed someone in an airplane bathroom.

Bautista, who was up next, revealed that he pulled a prank at school that involved uprooting several trees and planting them on the 50-yard line; Hiddleston got to show off his secret talent of playing the spoons after sharing that he learned how to play them at a bar; Gurira opened out about collecting vitamins — not the Flintstones kind.

[Found Out How Well](https://peplux.cc/category/malcv9yw)

The last question was about Ruffalo, who said he lost his virginity at the second hole of a golf course in Virginia Beach.

At the end of the game, Hiddleston, Johansson, and Ruffalo were tied with three points each, and had to answer one final question to win the game. Kimmel asked them to name all six infinity stones — and Hiddleston was the clear winner after naming them all correctly.

[Well They Know](https://science.ketoacv-gummies.com/blog/6301851097.html)

In the end, the cast members each got to take home some bonsai trees, one for each question they got right, and Hiddleston also won a brand-new lawnmower. The cast also got to draw their own Avengers characters during a commercial break, which will be auctioned off on a later date for charity.
