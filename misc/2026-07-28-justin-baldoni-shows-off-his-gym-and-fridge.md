For some Hollywood figures, shaking their pre-fame habits isn't so easy. Justin Baldoni, star of CW hit Jane the Virgin and director of upcoming film Five Feet Apart (dropping March 15), hasn't been able to ditch the thriftiness he developed as a young actor.

[Baldoni Shows Off His](https://leanorbit.dns0663.com/blog/3782523754.html)

Unlike other celebrity gyms stocked with sleek equipment, Baldoni’s home fitness room is built primarily from Craigslist purchases, as the actor hasn't quite graduated from the penny-pinching habits from his pre-success days.

[Off His Gym](https://science.ketoacv-gummies.com/blog/8769967227.html)

That doesn't make the space any less his own. Baldoni says he had a hard time making time for himself as his career has blossomed, so there’s a reason he calls his home gym his “sanctuary.” For his own sanity, he needs at least two gym sessions a week. “This is where I go to find my center, disconnect from the world, and to kick my own ass.”

Baldoni admits that in his early twenties his workouts focused primarily on aesthetics—exercises that made him look good. After having kids and feeling “like an old man,” Baldoni turned to what he calls “functional” fitness, including bodyweight and pool work. One of his favorite Craigslist buys, for example, is his inversion table. “I’m a man who has destroyed his body, and so just a minute every night on this thing, stretching myself out is huge.”

That wellness protocol has served him well in recent years. Baldoni had only 26 days to shoot his upcoming film Five Feet Apart—and then only six weeks before he needed to be back on set to shoot his breakout role on the final season of Jane the Virgin. He knew he would need to pull double duty.

So he did what any budding director would: He set up an editorial suite four dressing rooms down from his own, spending the next three-and-a-half months TV acting and then rushing down the hall to edit his directorial feature in between scenes. He didn’t sleep much, he admits. “I needed time for my family, wife, and kids, all while balancing the show and movie. It was a pretty intense period. I definitely have some grey hairs now.”

Baldoni says he’s trying to stick close to paleo for his diet. He pulls out a loaf of paleo bread from his fridge: “It’s grain free, gluton free, paleo, non GMO, kosher, dairy free, soy free and preservative free… so basically it’s like eating air,” he jokes. The actor also says he’s given into trends like drinking celery juice and will occasionally consume dairy like cashew yogurt. “It sounds disgusting, but I like it.”
