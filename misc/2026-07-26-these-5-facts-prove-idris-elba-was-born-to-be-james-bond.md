He Can Drive

You might have missed it between his movies and acclaimed TV shows, but Elba is a decent driver. He broke the UK land speed record with a 180.361 mph drive in a Bentley Continental GT Speed in 2015 for his Discovery Channel miniseries Idris Elba: No Limits. And he had a series on BBC America entitled King of Speed which saw him trying to master just about anything with a motor and wheels. Elba would do just fine wheeling an Aston around.

He Can Tap Into a Dark Side

Whether or not Ian Fleming intended it, when James Bond is portrayed on the big screen, he's got plenty of angst, brooding and some inner demons he's wrestling with. Sure, there's a splash of panache here and there and and some dry wit to round out the character, but there's a lot of darkness that can surround the world's most known spy. Elba's nailed that ethos before, starting with The Wire, where he took inspiration for his role of a drug dealer from his actual time spent dealing drugs. Later with Luther, Elba brilliantly plays a tormented anti-hero of a British cop, struggling to survive the doldrums of daily life. He can bring the hint of darkness to Bond's eyes, no problem.
