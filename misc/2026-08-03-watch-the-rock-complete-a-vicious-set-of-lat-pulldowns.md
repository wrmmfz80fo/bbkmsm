Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson has, in his own words, been "training [his] ass off" over the last several months, both as a means of staying positive and proactive during the pandemic, and also to prepare for filming his next two projects, heist movie Red Notice and DC's Black Adam.

The movie star just showed off his huge arms and core strength in a brief workout video from his home gym, known to all as the Iron Paradise. In the clip, he performs a set of intense, physically challenging pulldowns.

"The Rock is using a Hammer Strength lat pulldown machine, and he's essentially doing what I'd consider a technique dropset," says Men's Health fitness director Ebenezer Samuel C.S.C.S. "He's leading off with a demanding alternating half-iso pulldown, maintaining an isometric squeeze on one side, while doing a rep on the other. This ratchets up time-under-tension while also increasing core demands; his abs have to work extra-hard to keep his torso square to the front. After he's done those, he exhausts his lats with some eccentric reps, getting a slight "rock" to pull the weight down, then slowly lowering the weight back to the start. It's a pretty vicious series."

Johnson's form on the pulldowns is also noteworthy; he avoids wrapping his thumbs around the machine handles, meaning the shoulder won't be as irradiated, helping with lat engagement.

"It's not something the general population should do all the time," says Samuel, "but, both for functional reasons and lat development reasons, it's worth mixing in this thumbless grip here and there. If you're pulling yourself up, say, a ledge or a heavy tree branch, you may or may not be able to wrap your thumbs around it. So practice both over the long-term course of your training."

The post also included a first look at the Project Rock 3, the latest in Johnson's line of training shoes created in partnership with Under Armour. "Gym testing them out to the bone because when it meets my standards — it’s ready for YOU," he wrote.

It's the first weight room video Johnson has posted in a while; back in April, he said he would be putting a stop to his regular gym content as he understood that unlike him, most people haven't had access to workout equipment during lockdown. "It would make me feel like an asshole," he said.
