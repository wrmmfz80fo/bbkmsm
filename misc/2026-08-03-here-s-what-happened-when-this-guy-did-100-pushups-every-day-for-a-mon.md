# Here's What Happened When This Guy Did 100 Pushups Every Day for a Month

YouTube's Simple Man posts regular videos documenting his progress as he tries out new diets and physical fitness challenges, but like many people in lockdown, this spring he was feeling sluggish and unmotivated. In an attempt to get back into better shape, he decided to take on a popular YouTube fitness challenge: 30 days of 100 pushups.

(He's also inviting others to take part in their own 30-day pushup challenge to raise money for St. Jude's Children's Hospital — he explains all of the details on how to get involved in the video.)

Throughout the month, he includes variations on the pushups, performing some of them as decline pushups and also adding a weighted backpack (and occasionally a child on his back) to his reps during the later stages to make the reps more difficult.

On the very first day of the challenge, he maxes out at 47 reps in one set, and completes the rest of his pushups separately. By the final day, he is able to do 75 consecutive pushup reps without stopping, improving his max by 28. He also sees some physical results, with an increase in visible definition in his chest and some muscle growth in his upper arms.

Upon completing the challenge, he has some advice to offer anybody thinking of trying 30 days of 100 pushups themselves. "On Day 1 you might do 100, then on Day 2 you'll be so sore you're going to wonder how you'll make it through the day. That's normal. That's how it was for me," he says. "Day 2 I was sore, Day 3 I was super sore. But that's going to wear off around Day 7 or 8. You have to push through it, but it's going to be difficult."

He also recommends taking as much time as you need to complete all 100 reps, spreading them throughout the day if you have to. "I think taking a longer rest actually helped me in the long run," he says, "I think it helped me get a little better endurance."
