The Role of Platelet-Rich Plasma in Treatment

Traditionally, Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) has been used to aid in the recovery of tendon and muscle injuries. It’s now being used to treat erectile dysfunction, given its success in boosting blood flow and enhancing sexual function through natural healing.PRP, derived from the patient’s own blood, contains platelets and plasma that release growth factors, promoting blood flow for cellular rejuvenation. When PRP (devoid of red blood cells) is specifically injected into the penis, it encourages the release of proteins that enhance blood flow to the area and triggers specific therapeutic properties, aiding in the formation of healthier penile tissue. This results in increased sensitivity, firmer erections, and overall improved sexual performance. Platelet-rich plasma injections play a critical role in P-Shot® treatments.
