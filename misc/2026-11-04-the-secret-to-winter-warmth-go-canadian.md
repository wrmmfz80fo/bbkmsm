# The Secret to Winter Warmth: Go Canadian

When it comes to staying warm, we could learn a few things from our neighbors to the north—which is why we ventured to the chill of Toronto to check out the latest jackets from the home grown coat company, Canada Goose.

[Secret to](https://skin.ketopantrylist.com/blog/6749744526.html)

The iconic fur-lined jackets can be seen on the streets of New York and Chicago, but nothing compares to their presence in their homeland. The jackets furnish the windows of the fashion district and can be spotted on both men and women alike, even on a mild fall day.

[Winter Warmth](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

There’s a reason for the loyalty: Canada Goose has been keeping Canada warm for over 50 years, and has remained a family owned company, synonymous with attention to detail and focus on quality. The coats are worn everywhere from the streets of Toronto to researchers in the South Pole—all because of the non-bulky fabric, coyote fur (optimal for wind protection), and superior insulation and warmth.

[Go Canadian](https://pepitverte.com/dgrhee4tlj/)

We learned something else on our trip across the boarder: Canadians live up to their friendly reputation. That’s right, the company gave us an exclusive look at their new collection, shown left.

Here are our top picks from the well-known brand.

The HyBridge Lite Jacket

The HyBridge Lite proves that down jackets don’t need to be bulky. The flexible, breathable fabric make this jacket as light as a feather, but as warm as a parka. Sport it solo if you’re hiking on a mild day, or under your professional peacoat for added insulation when the temperature takes a dive. ($450, Paragon Sports)

The Chilliwack Bomber

Fashion and function unite in the Chilliwack Bomber jacket. With (nonbulky) white duck down, an adjustable wire hood, and coyote fur lining, this jacket has the style of a timeless flight jacket, but the utility of a Canada Goose classic. ($545, Atrium NYC)
