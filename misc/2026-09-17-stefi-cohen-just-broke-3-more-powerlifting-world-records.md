# Stefi Cohen Just Broke 3 More Powerlifting World Records

[Broke More Powerlifting](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/2133890200.html)

[Just Broke More Powerlifting](https://benpepsi.com/)

Stefi Cohen just set a staggering three new all-time world records in powerlifting. At Hybrid Showdown II, a World Raw Powerlifting Federation-approved event which took place at Cohen's Hybrid Performance Method gym, she broke the existing records for the deadlift and barbell squat in her weight class, as well as for total lift.

Cohen was the lightest she's been in years, weighing in at just 114.2 pounds for the event (she usually lifts in the 123 pound weight class). She had deliberately cut 15 pounds, in order to be able to compete in a slightly lower weight class and grab some more world records.

She immediately set about crushing the competition, deadlifting an unprecedented 205 kgs (456 pounds), squatting 202.5 kgs (446 pounds), and bench pressing 102.5 kgs (226 pounds), for a record-breaking lifting total of 510 kgs (1,124 pounds). This brings her number of all-time world records in strength sports up to 25.

Hybrid Showdown II also included an appearance by bodybuilder and gymnast Jujimufu, aka Jon Call. While Juji is known for his feats of strength and acrobatics on YouTube, he wrote on Instagram that this was his first full powerlifting meet.

He ended the day on a squat max of 302.5 kgs (666.9 pounds), a bench press max of 182.5 kgs (402.3 pounds), and a deadlift max of 302.5 kgs (666.9 pounds). No new world records, but still, not bad for a newbie with an arm injury.
