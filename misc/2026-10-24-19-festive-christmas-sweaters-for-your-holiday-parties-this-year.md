The ugly sweater tradition started years ago to encourage people to wear the kind of classic sweater designs that were holiday cliche. You know, the kind of sweaters your mom would make you wear every December when you were a kid. We're talking bright fair isle patterns and knitted Christmas scenes. What's been lost in all the pop culture references is ugly Christmas sweaters were an excuse to wear over-the-top and vintage knitwear most of us wouldn’t normally wear in everyday life. It's rare we reach for second-hand anymore, but that's ok. Investing in one of the "ugly" sweaters on this list below will provide you with solid go-to wear for years to come—that way you're not buying some paper thin garment that falls apart after one use.

Ahead is our very best list of Ugly Christmas Sweaters this season. You’ve got fun, silly sweaters that will get smiles and some classic, ornate knitwear that will get nods. Some are only going to get worn to holiday parties. While others, from some of our favorite menswear brands, will be in your rotation all winter long.
