# A 3-Time CrossFit World Record Holder Shares What Keeps Her Motivated

Since it was founded in 2000, CrossFit has experienced explosive growth, with more than 13,000 gyms—known as "boxes"—now located all over the world. In that time, through competitive events like the CrossFit Games and the CrossFit Open, the fitness regimen has also produced its share of stars, such as "Fittest Man on Earth" winners Mat Fraser and Rich Froning Jr. Another athlete who counts herself among the fitness regimen's most elite participants is four-time CrossFit Games competitor Michelle Kinney, who now holds three certified Guinness World Records in the CrossFit arena. Her records include most weight lifted by Atlas stone lifts in one minute (1,189 lbs), three minutes (3,079 lbs), and most burpee pullups in one minute (19).

[Shares What Keeps Her](https://raazpeptide.com/4yousjjgzk/)

Kinney, now 36, whom ESPN once referred to as a "super athlete" in 2013, filmed a brief segment with Guinness last year during which she explained the strategy behind her three record-breaking lifts. She also explains how she got started with the competitive lifting program in the first place. "I had a friend tell me about a CrossFit gym in town, so I visited and I loved it. I loved the atmosphere, I loved being coached, and I love pushing myself," she says.

After sharing some basic advice—as well as some insight into the form she had to maintain for the records to count—Kinney gets into how she continues to find motivation and set new fitness goals. "It's important to challenge yourself regularly so you don't go backwards," she says. "Challenges are what wake me up in the morning and give me this vigor for life."
