The short time frame for the 7-Minute Blitz program wasn't just picked at random. Time-strapped individuals might want to consider this: A 2013 paper published in the American College of Sports Medicine's Health & Fitness Journal found that a 7-minute, bodyweight workout protocol can be effective.

The authors of the paper found that their busiest clients were able to make fitness gains using high-intensity circuit training (HICT) workouts that combined aerobic and resistance training in short, fast-moving sessions. No, speedy workouts like this won’t get you superhero jacked or ready for a CrossFit competition, but they have been proven to build strength and endurance while burning fat and improving important health markers like your VO2 Max.

You can also string 7-minute workouts together repeatedly. Have more than 7 minutes of free time? Do a 7-Minute Blitz workouts in a row (or the same work back-to-back).

There is one catch to all this: to make the most of the short time, you have to go all out. Like other interval-based training regimens, the key to HICT workouts is to maintain maximum effort, with minimal rest periods in between exercises. When you take on the 7-Minute Blitz workouts, remember that effort and intensity should be your guiding principles.
