# Ben Affleck Fires Back at Critics of His ‘Pooching’ Gut and Massive Back Tattoo

Say what you want about his back tattoo, but Ben Affleck is doing just fine, thank you very much.

On Thursday, Affleck took to Twitter to respond to a New Yorker article that essentially called him an overweight has-been with an ugly tattoo.

The piece, titled "The Great Sadness of Ben Affleck," reflects on a few photos that surfaced showing Affleck in Hawaii as he filmed his new movie, Triple Frontier.

"As his younger co-stars, the actors Garrett Hedlund and Charlie Hunnam, wrestled in the surf like purebred puppies, Affleck, who is forty-five, was photographed wading into the ocean carrying a small red life preserver, running in the shallow waters, and toweling off on the beach," it says.

And then there's this bit of tragic imagery:

"The tattoo—so gargantuan that the bird’s tail found itself dipping below the waistband of Affleck’s blue swim trunks—was plainly visible. In one image, the actor stands alone, looking off into the middle distance. His gut is pooching outward in a way that, in a more enlightened country like, say, France, would perhaps be considered virile, not unlike the lusty Gérard Depardieu in his prime but, in fitness-fascist America, tends to read as Homer Simpsonesque."

Other words used to describe Affleck's tattoo include "enormous" and "garish."

[Fires Back at Critics](https://pepgen.cc/category/8q283r)

To all of this, Affleck simply replied on Twitter: "@NewYorker I’m doing just fine. Thick skin bolstered by garish tattoos."

Fans were quick to jump to Affleck's defense, tweeting back their support for the Boston-born actor and writer.

Affleck's tattoo has been the subject of much intrigue since it first appeared in 2016. While making an appearance on Extra, Affleck lied and told host Mario Lopez the tattoo was "fake for a movie." Affleck added, “I actually do have a number of tattoos, but I try to have them in places where you don’t have to do a lot of cover up they get sort of addictive, tattoos, after awhile.”

When Affleck was spotted on the beach in March, it became abundantly clear that the tattoo was, in fact, very real.
