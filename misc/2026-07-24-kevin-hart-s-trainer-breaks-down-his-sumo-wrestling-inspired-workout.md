# Kevin Hart’s Trainer Breaks Down His Sumo Wrestling-Inspired Workout

Kevin Hart’s new YouTube show "What the Fit" wants to make two major points about fitness: a) you can turn pretty much any location into a gym, and b) fitness can actually be funny.

[Trainer Breaks Down His](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Both points were on full display during the show’s first episode, which debuts on the superstar comedian’s Laugh Out Loud Network on YouTube on Thursday at 1:00 p.m. EST.

Each episode will feature Hart and one of his celebrity friends taking on a demanding — and hilarious — fitness challenge, with the first episode pairing the Jumanji star with late-night host Conan O’Brien during a sumo wrestling lesson. Future episodes feature guests like Khloe Kardashian, James Corden, and Evander Holyfield.

Amidst the fitness hilarity, however, Hart and his longtime trainer, Ron Boss Everline, will do a short, accessible workout inspired by the episode’s theme. The best part: they promise we can all do the workouts at home.

“The whole premise is to show people how to move with what you have,” Everline, who has trained Hart for nearly five years, says. “It’s called ‘What The Fit’ for a reason. How can you make use of what you have, have fun, and work out all at the same time? You define your fitness. The world becomes your gym.”

MH recently asked Everline to break down his home workout from the first episode of “What the Fit.” He says to aim for two sets of 20 reps for each of the following exercises.

Get into position for a squat, but use a stance that’s wider than hip-width, and point your toes outward. Keeping your chest up, lower your torso until your thighs are parallel with the ground, then spring back up and repeat. “It targets the inner thigh and the hamstrings,” Everline says. Add a barbell to increase intensity. Just don’t break out into an impromptu dance battle like Kevin and Conan did.

Duck Walks

Crouch down in a squatting position, aiming to get your upper thighs parallel with the ground, and stretch your arms out in front of you for balance. While still crouched, take four total steps forward (two with each leg) and two back; that’s 1 rep. Boss vows that incorporating duck walks into your workout will aid your hip mobility—and strengthen your core, too.

High Knees

Everline calls high knees his “favorite most-hated exercise to do on an interval timer” to get his clients’ heart rate up and really break a sweat. Run in place, but focus on driving your knees as high as possible — ideally higher than your hips — on each stride. Forget reps; just go for as many as you can in 30 seconds.

Partner Challenge: Plank To Pushups With A Twist

[Kevin Hart](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/)

Staying true to “What the Fit’s” theme that fitness can be fun, Everline recommends this partner workout. Do a pushup, then hold a plank for as long as it takes your partner to jump over you laterally. After your partner’s done 20 jumps, switch places.

“It kind of motivates you and holds you accountable,” Everline says of doing a partner challenge. “The core dynamic between me and Kevin is every day we wake up and push ourselves. I’m challenging him, he’s pushing to be better than me and I’m like, ‘There’s no way you can outdo me, as little as you are!’”
