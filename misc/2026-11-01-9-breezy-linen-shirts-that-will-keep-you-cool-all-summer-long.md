What to Consider

Linen shirts are an essential wardrobe staple for the warmer months. Here are a few things to keep in mind when looking for the one that fits your style best.

Fabric Quality

[Cool All Summer Long](https://peply.cc/2db10u48gv/)

Look for linen fabric that feels soft, breathable and tough. Avoid material that feels paper thin and crunchy, with little to no softness. The linen should have a level of thickness to help with durability, ensuring your shirt holds its shape and stays soft over time.

Fit

Choose a fit that complements your body shape and personal style, whether it’s classic or slim. An ill-fitting shirt can feel restrictive or too loose, undermining its comfort. Some shirts can even be layered over a tank top or t-shirt.

Versatility

We highly recommend finding a linen shirt that you can get the most wear out of. If it can be dressed up and dressed down for various occasions, then it’s a winner. Linen shirts are versatile in their various styles. Beyond the traditional Oxford cut, you can find a linen shirt in button-down, henley, polo or tunic forms. Some come with camp or mandarin collars, ideal for a laid-back vacation vibe.

Care instructions

Following care instructions will help extend the life of the shirt, so you can enjoy it for many seasons. Linen can be delicate, especially when wet, and incorrect washing or drying can lead to shrinkage, fading or weakening of the fibers.
