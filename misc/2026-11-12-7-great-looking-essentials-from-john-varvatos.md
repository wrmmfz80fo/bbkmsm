# 7 Great-Looking Essentials from John Varvatos

The start of a new season is the perfect time to evaluate our wardrobes. We’re given the opportunity to consider fresh ideas, as well as fresh ways of dressing: Out with the old, in with the new. We’re able to curate a set of essentials, pieces that make us look and feel good for a variety of occasions, that we return to time and again.

But like so many things, that’s easier said than done. Unless and until, that is, we’re presented with John Varvatos’s new spring collection. Available at Bloomingdale’s, the line features styles that are ready and waiting to accompany us to all the springtime adventures in store. There are sleek trousers, pleated to perfection, that’ll go from the cubicle or corner office to the cocktail lounge seamlessly. There are jackets made of buttery or waxed leather that’ll heat things up on brisk nights. For those days when you’re looking to cool things down instead, look no further than the linen sweater polo and the short-sleeve shirt with an eye-catching print. And let’s not forget about the hoodie made of cotton with a bit of stretch—a layer as suave as it is snug.

Perhaps best of all, every one of these pieces can be mixed and matched in various ways. All told, they’re a testament to the John Varvatos brand, a label that’s long been celebrated for classic styles that pack a punch.

Check out the spring collection via our top selects below.

John Varvatos Brian Leather Jacket

Leather jackets may feel a dime a dozen nowadays, but this one is truly singular: Not only does it come in a cool earth-tone color, but it also features sleek panels and a buttery texture that feels as good as it looks.

John Varvatos Kian Dropped Tapered Fit Pants

A great pair of khakis is the cornerstone of any wardrobe, and the Kian is a cut above the rest—literally. From the front pleats to the silhouette that tapers ever so nicely, these pants are a step up from regular trousers, imbuing both ease and elegance.

John Varvatos Odin Linen Sweater Polo

Polo shirts are chameleons, the most adaptable of toppers. Depending on the event at hand, they can be dressed up or down. And the linen material and knitted surface quality of this version elevates it beyond standard styles.
