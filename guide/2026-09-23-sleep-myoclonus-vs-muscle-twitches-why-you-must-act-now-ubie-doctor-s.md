Sleep myoclonus vs. muscle twitches: What's the difference?

Sleep myoclonus refers to sudden, involuntary muscle jerks that occur only during sleep or as you're falling asleep. It's typically harmless and often experienced as a "sleep start" or hypnic jerk. Regular muscle twitches (fasciculations), by contrast, can happen anytime while you're awake and may stem from stress, caffeine, dehydration, nutrient deficiencies, or, in rarer cases, underlying neurological conditions.

Timing: Sleep myoclonus occurs during sleep; muscle twitches occur while awake.

Cause: Sleep myoclonus is usually benign; twitches have varied triggers.
